Working Paper NEW

Shared Dealers, Slow-Moving Investors: How Weak Treasury Auctions Reach Agency MBS (October 2026)

Abstract

Primary dealers absorb the Treasury supply that end investors leave at auction, and the same dealers intermediate agency MBS. Using 771 Treasury auctions, I measure weak demand by the yield concession, the high-minus-median accepted yield. Weaker auctions are associated with larger dealer awards and Treasury positions. The agency MBS spread then widens over about a week and fades, with no pre-auction movement, and the widening is larger when dealer capital is scarce. Its path tracks dealers' activities on MBS TBA market: near-delivery customer volume falls and recovers over the same window. Because end investors move slowly across markets, who absorbs a shock shapes how prices adjust across bond markets.

Working Paper

Correlated Demand and Beta Dynamics in Sustainable Investing (October 2025)

Abstract

Using U.S. active equity fund data from 2012 to 2024, this paper shows that correlated demand from sustainable investors amplifies systematic risk for stocks in their portfolios. I identify stocks persistently overweighted by sustainable investors as experiencing demand shocks. These stocks exhibit significant return beta increases beyond what fundamentals would predict, with effects concentrated during 2016–2020. I develop a novel measurement capturing the coordination of capital flows across sustainable investors, revealing a demand-driven channel through which thematic investing reshapes systematic risk.
