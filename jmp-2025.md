# 2025-2026 Job Market Papers: Macro-Finance and Asset Pricing

### 1. Monetary Policy, Communication, and Information Effects
* **Amodeo, F. (University of Chicago). (2026).** *Mind the Gap: Disagreement and Credible Monetary Policy.* [[Paper Link](https://sites.google.com/view/framodeo/research)]
    * This paper argues that interest rate disagreement between the Fed and the market acts as a "wedge" in the Euler equation, which dampens the effectiveness of monetary shocks and explains the "activity puzzle."
* **Fernández-Fuertes, R. (CEMFI & Banco de España). (2025).** *Monetary Policy Shocks: A New Hope: Large Language Models and Central Bank Communication.* [[Paper Link](https://www.bde.es/f/webbe/SES/Secciones/Publicaciones/PublicacionesSeriadas/DocumentosTrabajo/25/Files/dt2529e.pdf)]
    * This work uses a multi-agent LLM framework to extract probabilistic expectations from narrative Fed documents (Beige Books and Minutes) to identify policy surprises that are less noisy than traditional market-based measures.
* **Simoncelli, G. (Sapienza University of Rome). (2025).** *Information Effects of Monetary Policy and Bond Yields in Emerging Markets.* [[Author Page](https://giacomosimoncelli.github.io/)]
    * This study documents that unanticipated tightenings lower long-term yields and inflation expectations in advanced economies but raise them in emerging markets due to the informational content of the surprise.

### 2. Fiscal Policy, Government Debt, and Market Impacts
* **Wiegand, C. (University of Amsterdam). (2025).** *The Effect of Fiscal Policy Shocks on Asset Prices.* [[Paper Link](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2023/program/paper/HKBF5T9N)]
    * By tracking the U.S. budget resolution process, this paper identifies high-frequency deficit news shocks and finds that they significantly raise yields, increase term premia, and reduce the "specialness" (convenience yield) of Treasuries.
* **Cavaleri, F. (University of Chicago Booth). (2025).** *Fiscal Insurance and the Pricing of Government Debt.* [[Paper Link](https://filippocavaleri.github.io/files/JMP.pdf)]
    * This paper models how government debt and taxes provide "fiscal insurance" against uninsurable idiosyncratic risks, showing that the relationship between debt supply and the risk-free rate is non-monotonic.

### 3. Sovereign Default, Debt Maturity, and International Reserves
* **Balestrini, T. (University of Minnesota). (2025).** *Sovereign Defaults: Maturity Choice and Conditionality.* [[Paper Link](https://www.teresabalestrini.com/research)]
    * This research demonstrates that restructurings under IMF programs result in larger maturity extensions and higher haircuts because fiscal conditionality acts as a commitment device to mitigate future debt dilution.
* **Rivera-Catalán, J. A. (University of Houston). (2025).** *Commodity Prices, Sovereign Risk, and Macroeconomic Stability.* [[Author Page](https://sites.google.com/view/jriveracatalan/)]
    * Calibrated to Chile, this model shows how cyclically adjusted budget balance rules can dampen the sensitivity of sovereign spreads to commodity price cycles, reducing procyclical borrowing.
* **Carreira, I. (University of Zurich / ETH Zurich). (2025).** *Default Risk and the Insurance Trade-offs: Bailouts or International Reserves?* [[Author Page](https://ines-carreira.github.io/)]
    * This paper identifies a non-monotonic relationship between IMF conditionality and reserves, where moderate conditionality encourages self-insurance (reserves) but very strict conditionality raises borrowing costs and forces deleveraging.

### 4. Asset Pricing, Ownership, and Market Power
* **Marrow, B. (University of Chicago Booth). (2024).** *The Pricing of Earnings News.* [[Paper Link](https://benmarrow.com/pdfs/JMP_draft.pdf)]
    * Using a demand-system approach, this paper shows that a stock’s response to earnings news depends on the heterogeneous price and earnings elasticities of its specific owners.
* **Ren, K. (Northwestern Kellogg) & Zhang, D. R. (Rice University). (2025).** *Market Power, Expectations, and Asset Prices.* [[Paper Link](https://ren-kevin.github.io/research/)]
    * This study documents that firms with low market power overreact the most to long-term growth news, leading to predictable return reversals and procyclical capital misallocation.
* **Barth, D. (Federal Reserve Board) & Zarra, N. (Federal Reserve Board / NYU Stern). (2025).** *Prime Broker Credit Supply and the Stock Market: Evidence on Hedge Fund Transmission.* [[Paper Link](https://www.nicholaszarra.com/jmp_website.pdf)]
    * This work shows that broad broker-dealer health shocks transmit to equity prices through the credit supply provided to levered hedge funds, triggering forced sell-offs and high price impact.
